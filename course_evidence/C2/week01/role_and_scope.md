# C2 Role and Scope

C2 owns packaging and delivery: the `_call_packager` method in `main.py`, plus `codeagent_packager.py`, `ProjectPackager`, and `DependencyGenerator` under `skills/CodeAgent/scripts/`. Work in `main.py` must stay inside `_call_packager`; other roles own the surrounding methods.

This retrospective describes the role assignment for the earlier course stage. It was written during Week 4 and does not claim that this file existed in Week 1 or that C2 implementation or tests had already been performed.
